# Víctor Andrés Gómez López

### Physiotherapist · Operations Leader · Health Data Builder

Head Physiotherapist at Optimo Gym in Riyadh 🇸🇦. PhD candidate in Physical Activity and Sport. Founder of [ADAPTY](https://adapty.global), currently in Misk Launchpad 10.0. I build human-supervised tools that make evidence-based physiotherapy faster, clearer, and more measurable.

**Full portfolio and writing:** [victor-gomez-portfolio.vercel.app](https://victor-gomez-portfolio.vercel.app), including **The Lab**, where I keep living notes on training, physiotherapy, data science, nutrition, and clinical AI.

## 📖 Background in one paragraph

Fourteen-plus years in health and performance. I started as a Les Mills Master Trainer for Latin America, worked as physiotherapist for Country Club de Bogotá premium athletes and the Club Atlético Newell's youth academy, and now lead physiotherapy at Optimo Gym (Armah Sports) in Riyadh, where I rebuilt the department and moved utilization from 10.9% to 62.5%. Along the way I completed a BSc in Physiotherapy, seven Master's degrees (Sports Rehab, Exercise Physiology, Sports Nutrition, Dietetics, MBA, and more), a Healthcare Management certificate from the University of Michigan, and the Stanford / Andrew Ng AI for Medicine Professional Certificate. I am currently in a PhD in Physical Activity and Sport.
