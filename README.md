## 📖 Background in one paragraph

14+ years in health and performance. Started as a Les Mills Master Trainer for Latin America. Physiotherapist at Country Club de Bogotá premium athletes and Club Atlético Newell's youth academy. Now Head Physiotherapist at Optimo Gym (Armah Sports) in Riyadh — rebuilt the department and moved utilization from 10.9% to 62.5%. Along the way I completed a BSc in Physiotherapy, seven Master's degrees (Sports Rehab, Exercise Physiology, Sports Nutrition, Dietetics, MBA, others), the Stanford / Andrew Ng **AI for Medicine Professional Certificate**, and I'm currently in a PhD in Physical Activity and Sport.
